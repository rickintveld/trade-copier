# Production Tips

## General Guidelines
- **Music:** Use trending audio or lo-fi beats for calmer reels, uptempo for fast hooks
- **Text on screen:** Always show the hook as text — 80% of viewers watch without sound
- **Format:** 9:16 (vertical), the first frame needs to grab attention immediately
- **Length:** Keep it under 30 seconds — Instagram and TikTok reward short watch times
- **Posting cadence:** 3-4 reels per week in the first month to build momentum

## Content Calendar Suggestion
- **Week 1:** Reel 1 (The Problem) + Reel 6 (Setup Tutorial)
- **Week 2:** Reel 4 (Prop Firm) + Reel 2 (The Speed)
- **Week 3:** Reel 7 (Before vs After) + Reel 3 (Risk Management)
- **Week 4:** Reel 5 (No Cloud) + Reel 10 (One Trade, Infinite Accounts)
- **Week 5:** Reel 8 (The Numbers) + Reel 9 (Mac & Windows)

## Best Practices
- Post at peak hours (8-9 AM and 6-8 PM in your audience's timezone)
- Use 3-5 relevant hashtags: #trading #mt5 #propfirm #forextrader #tradingtools
- Reply to every comment in the first hour to boost engagement
- Repurpose each reel for Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts
- Pin the best-performing reel to your profile
